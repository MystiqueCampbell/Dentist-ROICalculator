## 🔧 How the Calculator Works

### The 5 Key Inputs

The calculator needs 5 numbers from the dental practice to calculate ROI:

#### 1. **Monthly Inbound Leads**
**What it measures:** Total number of potential patients contacting the practice each month

**Includes:**
- Phone calls (answered AND missed)
- Website contact forms
- Live chat messages
- Text messages (SMS)
- Social media DMs
- Email inquiries

**Typical Range:** 30-150 leads/month
- Small practice: 30-50 leads
- Medium practice: 50-100 leads
- Large practice: 100+ leads

---

#### 2. **Current Conversion Rate (%)**
**What it measures:** Percentage of leads that successfully book an appointment

**Formula:** 
```
(Booked Appointments ÷ Total Leads) × 100 = Conversion Rate
```

**Example:**
- 60 total leads
- 18 booked appointments
- 18 ÷ 60 = 0.30 → **30% conversion rate**

**Typical Range:** 20-40%
- Below 25%: Poor (disorganized, overwhelmed)
- 25-35%: Average (industry standard)
- 35%+: Good (well-run practice)

**Why it's usually low:**
- Receptionist is busy with in-office patients
- Unclear phone script or process
- Long hold times
- Poor follow-up on inquiries
- Calls going to voicemail

---

#### 3. **Missed Calls Rate (%)**
**What it measures:** Percentage of calls that go unanswered or to voicemail

**Formula:**
```
(Missed Calls ÷ Total Calls) × 100 = Missed Call Rate
```

**Example:**
- 50 total calls
- 20 go to voicemail
- 20 ÷ 50 = 0.40 → **40% missed rate**

**Typical Range:** 30-60%
- 30-40%: Average practice
- 40-50%: Busy practice
- 50%+: Severely understaffed

**Common Causes:**
- Lunch breaks (12pm-1pm)
- Morning rush (9am-11am)
- After hours (5pm-8pm)
- Weekends
- Staff on vacation/sick
- Multi-tasking receptionist

---

#### 4. **Average New Patient Value ($)**
**What it measures:** Revenue generated from a new patient in their first year

**Includes:**
- Initial exam and cleaning
- X-rays
- Any immediate treatment
- Follow-up visits
- Additional procedures

**Typical Range:** $400-$1,500
- Basic cleaning practice: $400-$600
- General dentistry: $600-$1,000
- High-end/cosmetic: $1,000-$1,500+

**Example Breakdown:**
```
Initial Visit:
- Comprehensive exam: $150
- Full X-rays: $100
- Cleaning: $120
Subtotal: $370

Follow-up (within 1 year):
- Filling: $200
- 6-month cleaning: $120
Subtotal: $320

Total First Year Value: $690
```

---

#### 5. **Price Per Booked Lead ($)**
**What it measures:** How much YOU charge the practice per successfully booked appointment

**Your Pricing Options:**
- Conservative: $15-$20/booking
- Standard: $20-$30/booking
- Premium: $30-$40/booking

**Comparison to Alternatives:**
- Full-time receptionist: $35K-$45K/year ($2,900-$3,750/month)
- Your service at $25/booking × 40 bookings = $1,000/month
- **You're 65-75% cheaper!**

## 🧮 Understanding the Calculations

### Step 1: Calculate Current Performance

```
Current Booked Leads = Monthly Leads × (Conversion Rate ÷ 100)
Current Revenue = Current Booked Leads × Patient Value
```

**Example:**
- 60 leads × 30% = **18 booked appointments/month**
- 18 bookings × $600 = **$10,800 current revenue**

---

### Step 2: Calculate AI Performance

#### A. Recover Missed Calls
```
Recovered Leads = Monthly Leads × (Missed Calls % ÷ 100)
Total Leads with AI = Monthly Leads + Recovered Leads
```

**Example:**
- 60 leads × 40% missed = **24 recovered leads**
- 60 + 24 = **84 total leads with AI**

**Why this matters:** These 24 leads were calling but got voicemail. Now AI answers them!

---

#### B. Improve Conversion Rate
```
Improved Rate = Current Rate + 25%
(Capped at 85% maximum)
```

**Why +25%?**
- AI answers 100% of calls (vs. current 60%)
- Instant response (no hold time)
- Available 24/7 (nights, weekends)
- Never has a "bad day"
- Consistent, professional responses
- Books appointments immediately while patient is engaged

**Example:**
- 30% current + 25% boost = **55% new conversion rate**

---

#### C. Calculate New Revenue
```
Booked Leads with AI = Total Leads × (Improved Rate ÷ 100)
New Revenue = Booked Leads with AI × Patient Value
```

**Example:**
- 84 leads × 55% = **46 booked appointments**
- 46 bookings × $600 = **$27,600 new revenue**

---

### Step 3: Calculate ROI

```
Monthly Cost = Booked Leads with AI × Your Price Per Lead
Revenue Increase = New Revenue - Current Revenue
Net Gain = Revenue Increase - Monthly Cost
ROI % = (Net Gain ÷ Monthly Cost) × 100
First Year Gain = Net Gain × 12
```

**Example:**
- Monthly cost: 46 × $25 = **$1,150**
- Revenue increase: $27,600 - $10,800 = **$16,800**
- Net gain: $16,800 - $1,150 = **$15,650/month**
- ROI: ($15,650 ÷ $1,150) × 100 = **1,361%**
- First year: $15,650 × 12 = **$187,800**

## 📞 Getting Data from Dentists

### The Discovery Call Framework

Most dentists **DON'T** track these numbers precisely. That's okay! Use this approach:

---

### Question 1: Monthly Inbound Leads

**❌ DON'T Ask:**
"How many inbound leads do you get per month?"
- Too vague, they don't know what "leads" means

**✅ DO Ask:**
"About how many phone calls do you get per day from potential new patients? Let's say on an average Tuesday or Wednesday?"

**Follow-up Math:**
- "So about 10 calls per day?"
- "That's roughly 10 × 22 business days = 220 calls per month"
- "Do you also get website inquiries or form submissions?"
- "Maybe 5-10 per month? Let's call it 230 total leads"

**If they say "I don't know":**
"No worries! Most practices your size get around 50-80 calls per month. Let's use 60 as a starting point and we can adjust."

---

### Question 2: Current Conversion Rate

**❌ DON'T Ask:**
"What's your conversion rate?"
- They have no idea what this means

**✅ DO Ask:**
"Out of every 10 people who call your office, how many actually book an appointment?"

**Listen for:**
- "Maybe 3 or 4 out of 10" → **30-40%**
- "Not many, honestly" → **20-25%**
- "Most of them" → **40-50%** (they're overestimating)

**If they say "I don't know":**
"That's totally normal! Industry average is about 30%, meaning 3 out of 10 callers book. Let's start there."

**Pro Tip:** They'll ALWAYS overestimate. If they say 50%, use 35-40% in the calculator.

---

### Question 3: Missed Calls Rate

**❌ DON'T Ask:**
"What percentage of calls do you miss?"
- Makes them defensive, they'll lie

**✅ DO Ask:**
"When you're slammed during lunch or at the end of the day, about how many calls would you say go to voicemail? Just roughly?"

**Alternative questions:**
- "Do calls ever go unanswered when your receptionist is with a patient?"
- "What happens to calls that come in after 5pm or on weekends?"
- "On a busy day, do you think you miss 1 out of 5 calls? Maybe more?"

**If they say "very few":**
"I totally get it - you run a tight ship! Most practices miss about 30-40% of calls even with good staff. It's just the reality of being busy. Let's use 35% for the calculation."

**Red Flags (High Missed Call Rate 50%+):**
- "It's just me and one receptionist"
- "We're slammed in the mornings"
- "Our person takes a full lunch break"
- "We close at 5pm sharp"

---

### Question 4: Average New Patient Value

**❌ DON'T Ask:**
"What's your average patient lifetime value?"
- Too complicated, they'll overthink it

**✅ DO Ask:**
"When you get a brand new patient in for their first visit - exam, cleaning, maybe X-rays - what's that typically worth? And do they usually come back for follow-ups in that first year?"

**Help them calculate:**
"So initial visit is about $300-400... and maybe they come back once or twice for $150-200 each time? That sounds like $600-700 total first year?"

**Ranges by Practice Type:**
- **Basic cleaning:** $400-500
- **General dentistry:** $600-800
- **Family practice:** $700-1,000
- **Cosmetic/high-end:** $1,000-1,500+

**If they say "I don't know":**
"No problem! For a general dentistry practice, first-year patient value is typically $500-700. Let's use $600 and you can adjust."

---

### Question 5: Your Pricing

**Don't ask them - YOU set this!**

**Your Pitch:**
"Here's how my pricing works - I only get paid when you get a booked appointment. No monthly fees, no contracts. If I don't book anyone, I don't make a penny. Fair?"

"My rate is $25 per successfully booked appointment. So if I book you 30 new patients this month, that's $750. But you're making $18,000 from those 30 patients, so you're netting $17,250."

**Adjust based on their volume:**
- Small practice (<30 bookings/month): $20-25
- Medium practice (30-60 bookings/month): $25-30
- Large practice (60+ bookings/month): $30-35

## 💬 The Complete Sales Script

### Opening (After Initial Introduction)

**You:** "Before I show you what we can do, I need to understand your current situation. Mind if I ask you a few quick questions? Should only take 2-3 minutes."

**Them:** "Sure, go ahead."

---

### Question #1: Lead Volume

**You:** "First, about how many phone calls do you get per day from people wanting to schedule or ask about services? Just roughly - on an average Tuesday or Wednesday?"

**Them:** "Oh, I'd say... maybe 8-10?"

**You:** "Okay, so about 10 per day. That's roughly 200-220 calls per month. Do you also get website inquiries, like contact forms or chat messages?"

**Them:** "Yeah, maybe a handful per week."

**You:** "Perfect. Let's say 230 total leads per month. Make sense?" *(Write this down)*

---

### Question #2: Conversion Rate (Tricky One!)

**You:** "Now here's an interesting question - out of those 10 daily calls, how many actually end up booking an appointment? Like, if 10 people call today, how many are on your schedule by the end of the call?"

**Them:** "Hmm... probably 3 or 4? Sometimes more, sometimes less."

**You:** "So about 30-40%. That's actually pretty typical. Most dental practices are in that range." *(This normalizes it, removes defensiveness)*

**You:** "Let me ask this - why do you think the other 6-7 people DON'T book?"

**Them:** *(This is where they'll reveal problems)*
- "A lot of them want to think about it"
- "Some are just price shopping"
- "Some go to voicemail and never call back"
- "My receptionist is sometimes too busy to follow up"

**You:** "Got it. So let's use 35% for the calculator - does that feel about right?"

---

### Question #3: Missed Calls (The Money Question!)

**You:** "Now, be honest with me - when you're slammed at lunchtime, or at the end of the day, or your receptionist is helping someone in the office... how many calls would you say go straight to voicemail?"

**Them:** "Oh man, more than I'd like to admit."

**You:** "Don't worry, every practice deals with this! I'm not judging. Would you say it's 1 out of 5 calls? 1 out of 3?"

**Them:** "Probably closer to 1 out of 3, yeah. Maybe 40%?"

**You:** "40% is actually common for busy practices. And think about this - what time do most people call to book dental appointments?"

**Them:** "Probably... lunch breaks? Or after work?"

**You:** "Exactly! 12-1pm and 5-7pm. And that's exactly when you're closed or slammed. So you're missing the highest-intent calls. That's the big opportunity here."

---

### Question #4: Patient Value

**You:** "Quick question about revenue - when a brand new patient comes in for their first visit, what's that worth to you? Like the exam, cleaning, X-rays, any immediate treatment?"

**Them:** "First visit is usually $300-400."

**You:** "And do they usually come back for another cleaning or treatment within the first year?"

**Them:** "Yeah, most come back at 6 months for another cleaning. Some need fillings or other work."

**You:** "So would you say a new patient is worth about $600-800 in the first year, all-in?"

**Them:** "Yeah, I'd say $700 is a safe average."

**You:** "Perfect. Let's use $700." *(Write it down)*

---

### The Calculator Reveal

**You:** "Okay, I'm going to plug these numbers into my ROI calculator and show you something pretty eye-opening. Give me 30 seconds..."

*(Enter the numbers into the calculator live while they watch)*

**You:** "Alright, here's what I'm seeing..."

---

### Presenting the Results

#### Start with the Problem

**You:** "Right now, with 230 leads and a 35% conversion rate, you're booking about 80 appointments per month. At $700 per patient, that's $56,000 in monthly revenue from new patients."

**Them:** "Yeah, sounds about right."

**You:** "But here's the thing - you told me 40% of calls go to voicemail. That's 92 people per month who WANTED to call you but couldn't get through. We're not even counting the ones who called, got voicemail, and just called your competitor instead."

---

#### Show the Opportunity

**You:** "Now, if we recover those 92 missed calls with a 24/7 AI receptionist, you'd have 322 total leads instead of 230. And because AI answers instantly, never puts people on hold, and books appointments in real-time, your conversion rate goes up to about 60%."

**You:** "That means you'd book 193 appointments per month instead of 80. At $700 per patient, that's $135,100 in monthly revenue."

**Them:** "Wait, seriously?"

**You:** "Yeah. That's an additional $79,100 per month. Nearly $950,000 per year."

---

#### Address the Cost

**You:** "Now, here's my pricing: I charge $25 per successfully booked appointment. So if I book you 193 appointments, my fee is $4,825 for the month."

**Them:** "That seems expensive..."

**You:** "Let's look at it this way - you're making an EXTRA $79,100. After my $4,825 fee, you net $74,275 per month. That's $891,300 in the first year."

**You:** "Compare that to hiring another full-time receptionist at $40,000/year. I'm giving you 24/7 coverage, perfect reliability, instant booking, and you only pay when I deliver results. Plus I'm 3X more effective because I never sleep, never call in sick, and answer every single call."

**You:** "Your ROI is 1,538%. That means for every dollar you pay me, you make $15.38 back. Does that make sense?"

---

### The Close

**You:** "Here's what I suggest - let's run a 30-day pilot. I'll integrate with your calendar, handle all your incoming calls and inquiries, and we'll track exactly how many appointments I book. You pay nothing upfront. After 30 days, we'll look at the numbers. If I didn't deliver, you walk away. Sound fair?"

**Them:** "What's the catch?"

**You:** "No catch. I'm confident because I've done this for [X] other dental practices. I know the numbers work. The only requirement is that you have at least 30-40 leads per month, otherwise there's not enough volume to make it worth it for either of us."

## 🛡️ Handling Objections

### Objection 1: "That's too expensive"

**Response:**
"I totally understand - $25 per booking feels like a lot at first glance. Let me reframe it: You're paying $25 to make $700. That's a 2,700% return on every dollar. Can you think of any other investment in your practice that gives you that kind of return?"

**Alternative:**
"Let's compare: A full-time receptionist costs $40K/year plus benefits - that's $3,500/month whether they book 10 appointments or 100. With me, if I book you 50 appointments, you pay $1,250 and make $35,000. You're paying 3.5% of revenue vs. a fixed salary. Which makes more sense?"

---

### Objection 2: "Our conversion rate is already really good"

**Response:**
"That's awesome! But let me ask - what about the 40% of calls going to voicemail? Even if your conversion rate is 50%, missing 40% of calls means you're only converting 30% of total opportunities. That's where we make the biggest impact - capturing those missed calls."

---

### Objection 3: "We can't afford it right now"

**Response:**
"I hear you, but think about this: You're currently losing [calculated amount] per month from missed calls alone. That's real money walking out the door. My service costs [monthly cost], which means you're losing [10X their cost] by NOT doing this. Can you really afford NOT to fix this?"

---

### Objection 4: "Our patients want to talk to a real person"

**Response:**
"I completely agree - and that's exactly what they'll get. Our AI sounds completely natural and has real conversations. Patients don't even know it's AI unless we tell them. But here's the difference: while your receptionist can only talk to one person at a time, my AI handles 50 calls simultaneously. So EVERY caller gets immediate attention from a 'real person.' No hold times, no voicemail."

---

### Objection 5: "We need to think about it"

**Response:**
"Of course! But while you're thinking, let me calculate what that costs: Every day you wait is [daily loss amount]. Every week is [weekly loss amount]. I'm not trying to pressure you, but I want you to understand the opportunity cost. How about this - let's schedule a quick 15-minute call in 3 days to review your decision. Does Tuesday or Wednesday work better?"

## ✅ Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

### The 5-Minute Qualification Call

```
1. "How many calls per day?" → Calculate monthly
2. "How many book out of 10 calls?" → Get conversion %
3. "How many go to voicemail?" → Get missed call %
4. "What's a new patient worth?" → Get patient value
5. Input into calculator → Show results
6. Close with 30-day pilot offer
```

### Red Flags (Don't Waste Time)

❌ Less than 20 leads/month (not enough volume)
❌ Already using a similar service (hard to switch)
❌ "We'll never use AI" (ideological objection, won't convert)
❌ Brand new practice (not established, unpredictable volume)

### Green Lights (Great Prospects)

✅ 50+ leads per month (good volume)
✅ "We miss a lot of calls" (self-aware problem)
✅ Growing practice (expansion mindset)
✅ Multiple locations (scale potential)
✅ High patient value $700+ (more profitable for both)
